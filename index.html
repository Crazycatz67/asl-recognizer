Hand gestures — how to do them
🖐️ Delete a letter — sweep one open, spread hand quickly sideways, like wiping a whiteboard.
🖐️🖐️ Copy — bring both open hands together, like grabbing the text out of the air. Lands on your clipboard (Ctrl/Cmd + V anywhere).
🖐️🖐️ Paste — pull both open hands apart, like setting the text back down.

Every gesture needs the fingers spread apart — a letter shape won't trigger them.

How to spell
  • Add a letter — make the handshape and hold it still until the ring around the letter fills (about half a second).
  • Next letter — just change to the next handshape; no need to pause.
  • Same letter twice (the two L's in HELLO) — lower your hand or give it a clear bounce, then make it again.
  • Space between words — lower your hand and pause about a second, or tap Space. There is no hand sign for a space here.
  • Delete / Copy / Paste — see “Hand gestures” above, or use the buttons (Backspace and Space also work on a keyboard).
  • J and Z — draw the stroke in the air, the same as in Practice.
  • Clear — button only; wipes the whole line.
